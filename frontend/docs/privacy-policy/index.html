FitFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

FitFlow is an AI-powered fitness companion designed to help users manage workouts, nutrition, progress and fitness-related community activities. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when using the FitFlow application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, FitFlow may collect information such as:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Fitness goals and workout preferences.
  • Workout and progress information.
  • Nutrition and meal information entered by the user.
  • Water intake and habit tracking information.
  • Community posts, challenges and other user-generated content.
  • Technical information required to operate and improve the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide personalized workout recommendations.
  • Generate AI-assisted fitness plans.
  • Track workouts, nutrition and progress.
  • Provide community and challenge features.
  • Improve application functionality and user experience.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

3. AI Processing

FitFlow may use artificial intelligence to provide personalized fitness recommendations based on information supplied by the user. AI-generated recommendations are provided for general fitness purposes and should not be considered medical diagnosis, treatment or professional medical advice.

4. Health and Fitness Information

Users may enter fitness, nutrition and wellness-related information. Users should only provide information they are comfortable sharing. FitFlow is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new exercise or nutrition program when appropriate.

5. Community Features

FitFlow may provide community features such as posts, challenges and leaderboards. Information shared through public or community features may be visible to other users. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through public areas of the application.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested services, meet legitimate operational requirements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, users may have rights to access, correct, delete or request information about their personal data. Requests can be made through the application's designated support contact.

9. Children's Privacy

FitFlow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where such consent is required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FitFlow's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will include a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the FitFlow project support team through the contact information provided with the application.
